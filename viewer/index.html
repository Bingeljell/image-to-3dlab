          silhouette.

        TRELLIS.2 input guidance
        Flat/vector-style artwork may produce nearly black or incorrect materials.
          Prefer a photograph or softly lit 3D-style render. TinyCLIP provides an advisory
          check after upload; please also eyeball the image.
        Why?
        

      


      

        

          

        

          
          Advanced: mlx routes attention through MLX's fused Metal kernel. Only worth it at 1024 and above — attention cost grows with the square of the token count, and at 512 the stock path is already fast enough that mlx makes no measurable difference. At 1024 it cut sampling by about 3x. Output is unchanged: at a fixed seed, all three settings produce visually identical assets. Needs scripts/patch_trellis_mlx_attention.py applied and mlx installed in the vendor venv.

        

        

          Advanced: remesh is fixed demo-faithful, so this is a cap rather than a target.

        

          

        
      


      

        

        

        

          
          Higher is more detail and more GPU memory.

        

          
          More views cover more of the model and take longer.

        

          

      


      

        

          
          Pixal3D's single-image mode runs at 1024 only: there is no 512 texture
            model, and 1536 is refused for single images.

        

          
          8 looks the same as 12 on most models and runs 15-30% faster. Small
            markings on the back can come out softer. Needs the steps patch; without
            it, runs use 12.

        

        

          
          Pixal3D conditions on pixel-aligned features projected through a camera, so
            single-view input needs one. 0.3491 is 20°, the rig it was designed around.

        A picture with a transparent background goes straight in; anything else has
          its background cut out first (a few seconds). No repaint stage needed.
      


      

        

          

        

          

        

          

        

          
          -1 = no target (SF3D's own default density).

      


      

        

          
          512 is the evidence-backed default (2026-08-18 sweep); 1024 is smooth but very slow to remesh/paint.

        

        

          Hard ceiling ~500,000 — the paint stage's xatlas UV-unwrap step has a confirmed wall between 500k-700k faces (700k crawled; 1M never finished).

        

        

          

        

        

          

      


      

        

          
          2.0 is the default and the cleanest. 2.0-turbo is about twice as fast but
            can leave small dents. 2.1 is slower and not recommended.

        

          

        

        

          

        

          Same xatlas wall as the other Hunyuan backend — keep at or under 500,000.

Speed

      
How long a run takes depends on your machine and the picture. The progress bar shows
        each step as it happens; an NVIDIA card is usually faster than a Mac.

      
On a Mac, TRELLIS.2 runs about twice as fast with Attention backend set to mlx.

    


    

      
Known issues

      
    
        
  • Hunyuan's paint stage stalls above ~500,000 faces. Keep decimation_target at or under 500,000.
    • 
        
  • TRELLIS.2 can get colours badly wrong on flat or vector-style art. Photos and softly lit 3D-style pictures work best. Input guidance.
    • 
        
  • NVIDIA: Pixal3D's ready-made build needs driver 575 or newer. On an older driver, the installer compiles it instead if the CUDA toolkit is installed.
    • 
        
  • Windows has had limited testing. More testers wanted: tell us how it went.
    • 
      

    


    

      
Power-user notes

      
    
        
  • tail -f output/<folder>/run.log shows the same progress as the browser.
    • 
        
  • Tick debug to keep a run's manifest, textures and in-between meshes, not just the final .glb.
